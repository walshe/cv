Bitcoin Bites - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Bitcoin Bites is an offline Bitcoin facts/trivia app. This page explains what data the app does - and does not - collect.

Summary

Bitcoin Bites does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline: there is no server, no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What's stored, and where

Everything the app remembers about your usage is stored only on your device, in a local database. None of it is ever sent anywhere. This includes:

  • Which facts you've marked as favorites
  • Your daily usage streak
  • Your reading progress through the guided and random fact sequences
  • A record of which daily notifications you've already been shown, so the app doesn't repeat itself

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data, since it never existed anywhere except your device.

Permissions

Bitcoin Bites requests one permission on Android 13 and later: POST_NOTIFICATIONS, used only to show a daily Bitcoin fact reminder notification. This permission is optional - if you decline it, or disable notifications later, the app remains fully usable.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and makes no network requests of any kind.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date above.
